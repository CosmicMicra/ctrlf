03 / WHY CTRL+F?
Because the most interesting part of a paper is often what it doesn't prove.

CTRL+F is a space for reading security research actively, not collecting summaries. We pair foundational ideas with modern AI systems, asking what changed, what stayed broken, and what evidence would change our minds.

Papers are linked to their original sources. Briefs distinguish reported findings from our interpretation, and prompts are invitations to think, not tests with a single right answer.
